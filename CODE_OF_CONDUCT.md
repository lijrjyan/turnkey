# Code of Conduct

Turnkey is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Contributors
should be constructive, assume good intent, protect privacy, and direct
criticism toward ideas, evidence, and artifacts rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
doxxing, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private
information without permission. Safety research is not an exception to these
requirements: examples and discussions must minimize harmful content and avoid
exposing private prompts, identities, or datasets.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a contact
method on their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive
personal information. Maintainers will review reports fairly, preserve
confidentiality where practical, and communicate outcomes when possible.
